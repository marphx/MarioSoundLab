Welcome to Mario's Sound Lab

This app is designed to help users learn how iconic synth, electric piano, and bass sounds are built. Choose a sound bank, play the two-octave keyboard, stack chords, shape the patch, add effects, and watch the motion display react to what you play.

1. Play the keysTap or click the piano. Row 2 can collapse when you need more room. Chord Stack lets you build a chord one note at a time.
2. Shape the sourceWave Blend, Detune, Sub Tone, Noise, Envelope, Filter, Drive, and Tone Tilt show how a raw patch becomes expressive.
3. Add motionThe Modulator is an LFO. It can move filter, amp, pitch, or pan to create wobble, tremolo, vibrato, and stereo drift.
4. Use effectsChorus widens, Flanger swooshes, Phaser swirls, Delay repeats, and Reverb creates space.
5. Watch the visualizerThe airy multicolor motion window responds to note, velocity, chord density, and output level.
6. Save your soundExport a Sound Lab preset JSON or bounce a WAV preview. For Logic Pro, drag the WAV into Quick Sampler/Sampler.

Emergency Reset: If a note ever sticks or the engine glitches, press the red Emergency Reset button. It force-kills audio, clears held notes, resets controls to the current patch, and rebuilds the engine.

DAW note: A browser cannot create a real AUv3, VST, or Logic instrument patch by itself. This app exports browser-safe presets and audio previews, while a true MIDI-playable DAW patch requires a dedicated plug-in build.

Welcome to Mario's Sound Lab

This app is designed to teach sound design through touch. Pick a sound bank, play the two-octave keyboard, stack chords, reshape the tone, and listen to how each control changes the final patch.

PlayUse the on-screen keys to perform notes and chords. Turn on Chord Stack to build layered harmony without holding every key.
DesignUse envelopes, filters, oscillators, effects, and the modulator to learn what makes a patch soft, bright, wide, glassy, gritty, or heavy.
VisualizeThe motion window reacts to the notes and velocity so users can see the sound breathe while they hear it.
ExportSave your custom preset as a Sound Lab JSON file and bounce a high-quality WAV preview for use in a DAW sampler.

Important DAW note: A browser cannot create a real Logic Pro, AUv3, VST, or instrument plug-in patch by itself. This app exports a high-quality WAV preview and a reusable Sound Lab preset. To make a true MIDI-playable Logic patch, load the WAV into Logic's Sampler or Quick Sampler, or wrap this sound engine later inside a dedicated plug-in app.
